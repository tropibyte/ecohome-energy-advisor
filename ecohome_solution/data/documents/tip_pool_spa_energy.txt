# Pool and Spa Energy Management

A pool pump can be one of the largest energy users in a home with a pool, often more than 1,000-2,000 kWh a year.

## Variable-speed pumps
- Pump power rises steeply with speed: halving pump speed cuts power to roughly one eighth, while moving half as much water. Running longer at low speed filters the same water with far less energy.
- ENERGY STAR certified variable-speed pool pumps use substantially less energy than single-speed pumps, often cutting pump energy by more than half.

## How long to run the pump
- Most residential pools need the full volume filtered about once a day; with a variable-speed pump that is often 6-8 hours at low speed, not 12-24 hours.
- Reduce runtime in cooler months when algae growth is slower.
- Keep filters and skimmer baskets clean; a clogged filter makes the pump work harder for less flow.

## When to run the pump
- Run the pump during solar hours (about 09:00-15:00) if you have solar, or overnight off-peak if you do not.
- Avoid running the pump into the 16:00-21:00 peak window; a 1.1 kW pump running two hours in the peak costs about $1.15 per day at weekday peak rates versus about $0.53 during solar-midday hours.
- Split runtime into two blocks if needed for chlorination: one midday solar block and one short overnight block.

## Heating and covers
- Evaporation is the largest source of heat loss from pools. A pool cover can reduce heating costs 50-70% and cut water loss and chemical use.
- Solar pool heaters (unglazed collectors) are among the most cost-effective uses of solar energy.
- Heat pump pool heaters are far more efficient than electric resistance heaters; set the temperature to 78°F or lower.

## Spas and hot tubs
- Keep the cover on and in good condition; a waterlogged cover loses much of its insulating value.
- Lower the temperature a few degrees when not in use for several days, and use economy mode to heat during off-peak or solar hours.

## Chemistry
- Balanced water chemistry reduces the filtration time needed to keep water clear.
